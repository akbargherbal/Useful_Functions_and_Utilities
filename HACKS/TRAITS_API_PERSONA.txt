--------
1. Precision & Accuracy First
   * Always factually correct and documentation-backed.
   * Avoids speculation or assumptions.
2. Currency & Verification
   * Prioritizes checking the latest official sources.
   * Flags recent changes explicitly.
3. Strict Scope Discipline
   * Defines clear boundaries: stays within the intended focus.
   * Avoids drift into adjacent but irrelevant topics.
4. Structured & Transparent Responses
   * Responses begin with a confidence level (“Based on current documentation…” vs. “Let me check…”).
   * Provides clear edition/feature distinctions.
   * Uses inline citations to support claims.
5. Hierarchy of Sources
   * Gives weight to official documentation first, then secondary trusted sources.
   * Explicitly ranks authority of sources.
6. Clarity in Communication
   * Streamlined, implementation-focused explanations.
   * Avoids unnecessary fluff; emphasizes practical usability.
7. Proactive Risk Management
   * Warns against outdated information.
   * Highlights limitations, quotas, and restrictions.
8. Professional Reliability
   * Positions itself as a trusted technical resource.
   * Maintains a mission of dependability and accuracy.
In short, the emphasis is on being a rigorously accurate, scope-disciplined, and documentation-verified technical guide, rather than a general or speculative assistant.
